🌴 DISCOVER THE BEAUTY OF SRI LANKA

Experience the true beauty of Sri Lanka on this unforgettable 6-day journey from 23rd Oct 2026 to 28th Oct 2026. Explore ancient heritage sites, enjoy an exciting elephant safari, experience traditional Ayurveda relaxation, and feel the warmth of authentic Sri Lankan hospitality.

From traditional Sri Lankan cuisine and spice gardens to cultural performances, iconic waterfalls, and city tours, every day offers something special. This tour is designed to give you a wonderful taste of Sri Lanka and create unforgettable memories. 🇱🇰🌴✨

🗓️ Detailed Itinerary

Arrival at Colombo International Airport at 11:30 PM on flight CX 611. Meet and greet at the airport, followed by a direct transfer to your hotel in Habarana for check-in and an overnight stay.

✈️ CX 611 Flight Arrival 🤝 Meet & Greet 🚗 Direct Habarana Transfer
Overnight Stay: Cassandra Culture Resort (Habarana) | Nights: Night 1 of 2

Morning: Breakfast at the hotel, followed by free time to relax after your late-night arrival.
Afternoon: Experience the famous Habarana Elephant Safari and immerse yourself in Sri Lanka's wildlife.
Evening: Rejuvenate with a traditional Sri Lankan Ayurvedic massage and enjoy a peaceful evening.

🐘 Habarana Elephant Safari 💆‍♂️ Traditional Ayurveda Massage 🌿 Morning Relaxation
Overnight Stay: Cassandra Culture Resort (Habarana) | Meals: Half Board (HB)

5:30 AM: Early morning departure to climb and explore the magnificent Sigiriya Rock Fortress, followed by a photo stop at a prime viewpoint. Return to the hotel for breakfast.
10:30 AM: Check out and transfer to Kandy. En route visits include Dambulla Golden Temple and a Spice Garden with lunch arranged by us. In Kandy, attend a Cultural Show and visit the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic.

🏰 Sigiriya Rock Fortress 🛕 Dambulla Golden Temple 🌿 Spice Garden & Complimentary Lunch 💃 Kandy Cultural Show 🛕 Temple of Sacred Tooth Relic
Overnight Stay: Thilanka Kandy | Meals: Half Board (HB) + Complimentary Lunch at Spice Garden

9:00 AM: After breakfast, depart for Nuwara Eliya. En route visits include Ramboda Falls, lush green tea plantations, a working Tea Factory tour, and mountain road viewpoints.
If time permits in Nuwara Eliya: Visit the historic Nuwara Eliya Post Office, Seetha Amman Temple, and Single Tree Hill Viewpoint before checking in to relax.

🌊 Ramboda Falls ☕ Tea Factory & Plantation Tour 📮 Historic Post Office 🛕 Seetha Amman Temple
Overnight Stay: Heaven Seven (Nuwara Eliya) | Meals: Half Board (HB)

After breakfast, depart toward Sri Lanka's beautiful south-west coast through scenic mountain roads, stopping at St. Clair's Waterfall viewpoint (time permitting). In the evening, visit a local Sea Turtle Hatchery to learn about conservation efforts and relax by the ocean.

🌊 St. Clair's Waterfall Viewpoint 🐢 Sea Turtle Hatchery 🏖️ Induruwa Beach Relaxation
Overnight Stay: Pandanus Beach Resort (Induruwa) | Meals: Half Board (HB)

Enjoy breakfast, check out, and transfer to Colombo for a city tour highlighting the Red Mosque (Jami Ul-Alfar Mosque), Independence Square, and other city landmarks.
Optional Experience: Scenic train ride from Mount Lavinia to Colombo Fort (timing permitting).

🕌 Red Mosque (Jami Ul-Alfar) 🏛️ Independence Square 🚆 Optional Mount Lavinia Train Ride
Departure Transfer: Transfer arranged to Airport / Next destination.
