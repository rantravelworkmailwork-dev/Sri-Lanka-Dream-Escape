🌴 DISCOVER THE BEAUTY OF SRI LANKA

Experience the true beauty of Sri Lanka on this unforgettable 6-day journey from 23rd Oct 2026 to 28th Oct 2026, where nature, culture, adventure, and relaxation come together. Explore ancient heritage sites, enjoy an exciting elephant safari, discover traditional village life, and experience the warmth of authentic Sri Lankan hospitality.

Travel through beautiful beaches, lush forests, misty mountains, and green tea plantations. Enjoy a scenic Madu River boat ride, visit a sea turtle hatchery, explore the historic Sigiriya Rock Fortress, and discover the cultural treasures of Kandy and Nuwara Eliya.

From traditional Sri Lankan cuisine and Ayurveda experiences to cultural performances, tea plantations, waterfalls, and breathtaking mountain views, every day offers something special. This tour is designed to give you a wonderful taste of Sri Lanka and create unforgettable memories with your loved ones. 🇱🇰🌴✨

🗓️ Detailed Itinerary

Early morning arrival and transfer to Habarana. Early check-in at the resort to rest and recover from your flight.

🌅 Early Arrival 🏨 Early Check-in
Stay: Priya Wimana Resort | Meals: Bed & Breakfast (BB)

Immerse yourself in authentic Sri Lankan rural life with a traditional village tour. Experience an elephant safari in Habarana and end your day with a relaxing Ayurveda massage.

🐘 Elephant Safari 🌾 Village Tour 💆‍♂️ Ayurveda Massage
Stay: Wewa Addara | Meals: Half Board (HB)

Ascend Sigiriya Rock Fortress in the morning. En route to Kandy, enjoy lunch and interactive activities at a Spice Garden. In Kandy, explore a famous local Gem Museum. and watch a traditional cultural dance show.

🏰 Sigiriya Rock 🌿 Spice Garden Lunch 💎 Gem Museum 💃 Cultural Dance Show
Stay: Hotel Thilanka | Meals: Half Board (HB)

Travel up into misty Nuwara Eliya. visit the Temple of Tooth in the morning and Stop by Ramboda Falls, tour a working tea garden and tea factory

🛕 Temple of Sacred Tooth 🌊 Ramboda Falls ☕ Tea Plantation & Factory
Stay: Misthill Rest | Meals: Half Board (HB)

Head down to the southwest coast to Induruwa. Enjoy an evening boat safari along the Madu River mangrove wetlands and visit a sea turtle conservation hatchery.

🚤 Madu River Boat Ride 🐢 Sea Turtle Hatchery
Stay: Pandanus Beach Resort & Spa | Meals: Half Board (HB)

Relax at an extra resort free of charge to rest before the wedding festivities start. At midnight, transfer directly to Mr. Amal’s house.

🏖️ Resort Rest (Free of charge) 🚗 Midnight Transfer
